Web projects I've managed for agency and freelance clients as a digital project manager and technical sales engineer, from discovery and scoping through launch and ongoing operations.

  • Project C.U.R.E. delivers donated medical supplies and equipment to health facilities around the world. I managed a full website redesign and build on WordPress, with custom blocks and custom graphics.

  • I managed intranet and extranet resource sites for Denver International Airport, including page building, custom integrations, and statements of work and scopes. I also led client discovery and account management.

  • Native Campervans rents fully outfitted campervans from Denver, Las Vegas, Salt Lake City, Phoenix, and Los Angeles. I managed the automation that connects their e-commerce store to Airtable and Salesforce, plus lead qualification through HubSpot.

  • Ubiquinol.org is a science-backed education site for Kaneka Ubiquinol. I managed a full website redesign on WordPress, with full WooCommerce store integration, Salesforce integrations, and graphic design.

  • I managed a full e-commerce website redesign for NewQ, including a new WooCommerce shop for Kaneka Ubiquinol supplements with filtering by dietary needs, quantity, dose, and brand.

  • C&IH provides industrial and occupational hygiene consulting. I managed a full website redesign on WordPress, end-to-end lead automation, and a full SEO rewrite of the site's content.
